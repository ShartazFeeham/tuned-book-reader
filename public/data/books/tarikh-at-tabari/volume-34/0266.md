- **Vardan.** *La domination arabe en arménie: extrait de l'histoire universelle de Vardan*. Tr. J. Muyldermans. Louvain and Paris, 1927.

  - *Irshād al-arīb ilā ma'rifat al-adīb (Mu'jam al-udabā')*. Ed. D. S. Margoliouth, *E. J. W. Gibb Memorial Series*, 6. 7 vols. Leiden and London, 1907–27.
